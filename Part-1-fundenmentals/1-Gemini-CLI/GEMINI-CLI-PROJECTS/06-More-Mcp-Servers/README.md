### 💡 Why Use These MCP Servers?

Each MCP server supercharges Gemini CLI by connecting it directly with real-world tools and cloud platforms — turning your AI assistant into a hands-on developer companion.  

Here’s what they bring to the table:

---

#### 🔥 **Firebase MCP Server**
- Enables Gemini CLI to interact with your Firebase projects in real-time.  
- Lets you **query, initialize, and manage Firebase** resources directly through chat.  
- Boosts productivity by skipping manual CLI work.  

---

#### 🧭 **Google Workspace MCP Server**
- Seamlessly integrates with **Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Gmail**.  
- You can literally *ask Gemini to create presentations, emails, or reports* using live data.  
- Ideal for automating office workflows and content generation.  

---

#### 🎨 **Google Gen AI Media Services MCP Servers**
- Unlocks creative superpowers — access **Imagen, Veo, Lyria, and Chirp** directly via Gemini CLI.  
- Generate videos, images, and audio through AI commands.  
- Perfect for developers exploring generative media APIs with minimal setup.  

---

#### 🗄️ **MCP Toolbox for Databases**
- Connects Gemini CLI to databases like **BigQuery** and others instantly.  
- Lets you run SQL queries, fetch data, and analyze results from chat.  
- Great for data engineers and analysts who want conversational data exploration.  

---

### 🌐 In Short
> These MCP servers extend Gemini CLI from a simple prompt tool to a **full-stack AI development environment** — bridging Firebase, Google Workspace, GenAI Media, and Databases right inside your workflow.
